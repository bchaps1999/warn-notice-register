# Draft: Ohio WARN source inventory request

**Status:** Draft for the project owner to send. No request has been sent.

**Recipient:** Ohio Department of Job and Family Services, Office of Workforce Development / Rapid Response records custodian

**Subject:** Public WARN notice inventory and posted source files, 2023–2025

Hello,

I am reconciling Ohio's publicly posted Worker Adjustment and Retraining Notification (WARN) notices against the original agency records. Please provide the agency's complete public inventory of WARN notices received or posted from January 1, 2023 through December 31, 2025, preferably as a spreadsheet or existing database export. For each entry, please include the agency notice or filing ID (if assigned), employer, affected site or county, date received, date posted, the public notice document URL or file name, and any status or link identifying an amendment, correction, withdrawal, or rescission. Please include any existing annual counts and the agency's definitions for whether those counts represent documents, filings, affected sites, or layoff/closure events.

If no such inventory exists, please provide the public index pages or directory listings used to post the notices for each year, along with any readily available files that map public PDFs to a notice ID or posting date. I am seeking public employer notices and index metadata; please exclude worker lists and personal contact information about affected employees.

Electronic records or links are preferred. If this request is better directed to another office, please let me know the appropriate custodian.

Thank you.

## Why the inventory is needed

The current source-only register includes Ohio agency annual tables for 2015–22 and a current 2026 capture. Individual official PDFs are available for 2023–25, but a collection of discoverable PDFs cannot prove that all filings for those years have been found. ODJFS's [PY 2024 WIOA annual narrative report](https://dam.assets.ohio.gov/image/upload/v1764957643/jfs.ohio.gov/owd/WIOA/docs/WIOAAnnualNarrativeReportPY2024.pdf#page=17) reports 67 WARN notices for 14 layoffs and 41 closures, including one closure that submitted 13 notices. That aggregate can check a later inventory, but it has a different time window and may count documents differently from the register's canonical events.

After receipt, preserve the original inventory and documents with hashes, reconcile every source row and amendment to the existing Ohio keys, and hold entries whose site or event identity remains ambiguous. Do not infer missing filings or worker counts from the aggregate.
