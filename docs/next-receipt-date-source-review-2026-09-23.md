Kentucky has filing URLs that may support sampled notice-date correspondence, but the dataset's `date_received` should not automatically become the filing's date.

## Nevada diagnostic work

The frozen Nevada CSV has 297 raw rows and only **one** `Received Date` in 2021. The current [Nevada agency page](https://detr.nv.gov/Page/WARN) links annual PDFs, and an earlier [official 2021 report](https://cms.detr.nv.gov/Content/Media/2021_updated_210201.pdf) lists `Received Date`, `Notice Date`, and `Effective Date` as separate columns. Its Food Source row has January 12, 2021 receipt and January 4 notice. The installed Nevada scraper projects only eight fixed columns and omits `Notice Date`; it also searches annual links by the phrase `WARN Act Notices`. These facts establish a source-coverage and projection gap, not a count of missing legal filings. Additional observations remain excluded until the source can support them.

The local `nv_agency_received_role_v1` correction leaves legal `notice_date` blank, preserves the raw and parsed receipt day in provenance, and uses that day only for the existing internal dedupe key. Against the frozen raw CSV it produced 297 successful normalized records, zero parse failures, 296 distinct keys, no keys added or removed versus v6, and zero nonblank legal notice dates. The one same-key raw pair remains an identity/version issue. The source-only BLN selector now excludes Nevada. Two full replays retain 86,435 total notices, 296 Nevada notices, 35,892 Nevada workers, and the same 39,164-row exception ledger. They remove the 295 previously labeled Nevada notice dates and three date-dependent links; the prior link claims are in the [Nevada link review sheet](nv-legacy-link-review-2026-09-23.csv). Matching fingerprints, exports, and timing outputs are recorded in the [v7 progress checkpoint](rebuild-progress.md).

The same-key pair is Elite Line Services, Las Vegas/Clark: two source rows with February 22, 2024 receipt and April 22 effective dates but affected totals of **76** and **75**. The current key treats them as versions; the public table alone does not establish whether this is a revised count or two separate observations. Keep both raw rows visible pending source review.

## Massachusetts source-format preview

The frozen current `raw/ma.csv` has 377 rows. Its `RECEIVED` column contains one parseable date in 372 rows, no parseable date in four, and two dates in one Sodexo/Suffolk University row (`07/07/2021 - (08/30/2021)`). The `DATE(S) OF LAYOFFS` column contains two or more date tokens in 125 rows, one in 237, and none in 15. The current candidate also has 173 FY2020 archive rows; that workbook projection labels column zero `Date Received`. Thus a Massachusetts correction must cover **both** the current raw table and the separately admitted archive, while preserving the archive's old key and its existing overlap accounting. Of 550 candidate MA notices, 546 currently have a canonical notice date and 524 enter provisional timing; none enters strict timing. Current-source rows carry 111 effective ends, whose interval/list interpretation must be retained and checked rather than erased by a notice-role fix.

There is a concrete current-row mismatch: the frozen GSK plc. row labels **July 16, 2025** `RECEIVED` and reports an October 4, 2025 to March 31, 2026 layoff range. The linked [agency-hosted GSK filing](https://www.mass.gov/doc/gskplc-07-16-2025-warn-notice/download) is dated **July 15, 2025**. That one-day difference confirms the received field cannot be treated as the filing's written notice date for this row. It is one verified example, not a Massachusetts-wide error rate or a claim about when every employee got notice.

The v8 correction covers both source paths. It preserves all 550 old Massachusetts keys/source IDs, 58,197 workers, 528 effective starts, and 111 ends; it clears 546 receipt values from legal notice. The FY2020 archive's old receipt-month overlap boundary is retained. Two full replays and every generated output match; see the [v8 checkpoint](rebuild-progress.md) and [state table](date-eligibility-state-summary-2026-09-23-v8.csv).

## Kentucky lineage boundary

The frozen `raw/ky.csv` and `backfill/raw/ky.csv` each have 828 rows with the same headers, including `date_received` and `date_effective`. Current-source normalization yields 828 rows and 806 distinct keys with zero parse failures; all 806 keys exist in the v7 candidate. The candidate has **1,287** Kentucky notices, leaving **481 candidate-only keys** attributed to conservative BLN gap filling. A direct role correction during raw normalization would change the notice-date cutoff/month occupancy used by BLN selection, so the 481 cannot be ignored. The [Kentucky Rapid Response manual](https://kwib.ky.gov/WIOA%20Planning%20and%20Policy/WIOA%20Planning%20and%20Policy%20Documents/23-010_Guidance_Rapid_Response_Layoff_Aversion.pdf) describes WARN notices received at state/local units separately from employer communications; the raw field name points to receipt, but filing-level dates have not yet been reconciled. Next implement a stable selection boundary and distinguish current-source receipt from BLN-only historical date transcriptions before changing canonical Kentucky dates. Exact source-row dispositions must remain in the exception manifest under the deterministic contract.

The current-source date field has 819 ISO midnight cells, seven blanks, and two unparseable texts (`N/A`, `November`). The `ky_agency_received_role_v1` projection preserves the 819 receipt days as agency facts, clears legal notice, and retains every one of the 806 old current-source keys with zero parse failures. The older BLN transcription's notice date remains a distinct lineage question.

That unguarded whole-pipeline diagnostic admitted **675 extra Kentucky notices** solely because clearing the raw notice date changed the BLN older-row cutoff and occupied-month screen. It produced 1,962 Kentucky notices and 305,937 affected workers versus v8's 1,287 and 214,798; the unguarded build is rejected. Among the 481 BLN-only v8 keys, 478 have a date pair and **299 have a negative interval**; 27 report exactly 1,900 workers. None has an exact folded-employer/effective-date counterpart in the 828-row frozen state table. These facts do not prove every transcription false, but no state filing identity or source-labeled notice role is available for safe admission. The guarded source-only rule therefore excludes Kentucky BLN rows as `source_identity_unresolved`, preserving them in the exception manifest. Two guarded replays agree: Kentucky falls to 806 notices and 106,866 workers, a loss of 481 notices and 107,932 workers relative to v8. This is a deliberate source-evidence limitation, not a hidden date parsing side effect. The [v9 date table](date-eligibility-state-summary-2026-09-23-v9.md) records the remaining timing denominators.
