# Illinois source-record identity transition (2026-09-23)

This is an isolated source-only candidate check, not a published database or a determination that each IEBS row is a separate legal WARN filing. The frozen Illinois public export includes an opaque `IEBS Id` on every one of its 4,871 rows. The prior normalizer keyed Illinois by employer, notice date, and location. That mutable key conflated eight pairs of distinct IEBS records: three pairs with identical canonical content were coalesced and five pairs became versions of the same notice. The new normalizer keys an Illinois source record as `IL:IEBS:<ID>` and preserves the ID and its identity basis in `source_details`.

The rule is deliberately bounded. It does not infer a date from the ID, merge related facilities, allocate workers, or decide whether two IEBS records describe one legal filing or layoff. Within one snapshot, an ID-less row or different content under one ID is held with a row-level exception. An identical duplicate may coalesce with a pointer. The live pipeline rejects an Illinois database with legacy or mixed keys even if that database has an older `clean-rebuild-v1` marker; an isolated rebuild or reviewed migration is required before live ingestion.

## Release disposition

This fixes a reproducible source-record conflation and makes future source revisions update one IEBS record. Illinois still has a stale source capture, unresolved BLN historical rows, and no source-backed determination of relationships among distinct IEBS records that may refer to the same event. The new candidate remains staged. A fresh agency capture, its pinned bytes, and a follow-up row-level comparison are required before accepting Illinois coverage for v1.
