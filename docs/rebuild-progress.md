## Objective and ownership

Build a defensible WARN dataset from pinned source evidence that can be rebuilt in a fresh environment. For each canonical notice, preserve its source observation, event identity, date roles and precision, affected site/worker interpretation, and the provenance of any decision. Publish a notice-to-effective-date research cohort only with an explicit eligibility rule and state/source denominators. Historical legal filer and parent relationships require dated evidence and distinct relationship types; unknown remains a valid result. A staged replacement of the published database, CSVs, and site is the final step after the contract's gates pass.

This workstream owns source coverage, mechanical parsing, date interpretation, event identity, temporal entity evidence, and end-to-end release validation. The supported build now excludes LLM calls, model decisions, and manual review queues under the [deterministic assembly contract](rebuild-contract.md). Its exception manifest is source-row accounting with explicit conservative exclusions, not a request for model or human adjudication.
