From the frozen packet, `Rscript`/base R counts reproduce 41 notices, 30 cases, 21/21 CA blank `location`, 41/41 blank `site_address`, eight distinct `source_url`, and two effective dates earlier than notice date. An independent read-only comparison found all 41 packet and worksheet rows agree on case, state, employer, dates, location, address, and count. The CA/TX/FL/NC/MD/WI/IN tests cited in `notes/validation.md` rehash archived agency files and verify the cited records. These findings are sample diagnostics. The affected-site discrepancy is a direct source conflict; the other items call for field definitions, improved extraction, or original-document review before a general correction to `warn-live`.
